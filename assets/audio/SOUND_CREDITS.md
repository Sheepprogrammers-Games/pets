# Animal Sound Credits

The game ships locally processed, shortened excerpts of these recordings:

- Dog: [Barking of a dog.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Barking_of_a_dog.ogg), CC BY-SA 3.0.
- Cat: [Meow of a Siamese cat - freemaster2.wav](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Meow_of_a_Siamese_cat_-_freemaster2.wav), CC0; and [Meow of a pleading cat.oga](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Meow_of_a_pleading_cat.oga), public domain.
- Rabbit and hamster source: [Rabbit oinks and squeaks.wav](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rabbit_oinks_and_squeaks.wav), CC0. The hamster cue is a shortened, pitch-raised excerpt.
- Poodle: [Puppy at a night.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Puppy_at_a_night.ogg), CC0.
- Fox: [Red Fox (Vulpes vulpes) (W1CDR0001529 BD12).ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Red_Fox_(Vulpes_vulpes)_(W1CDR0001529_BD12).ogg), CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Budgerigar: [Budgerigar chirping.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Budgerigar_chirping.ogg), public domain, recorded by mary905 and transferred to Wikimedia Commons by Fae.
- Cockatiel: [Nymphicus hollandicus call.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nymphicus_hollandicus_call.ogg), CC BY-SA 3.0, recorded by ZooFari.

Each animal has three excerpts. All excerpts were converted to mono MP3, loudness-normalized, trimmed, and faded for in-game playback.
